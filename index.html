About me

I'm Dilson Freitas, a designer based in Rio Grande, Brazil. I work on visual identities, social media and digital interfaces. I like the part of a project where choosing a typeface or moving a single line changes how the whole thing feels.

I study Computer Engineering at FURG and design communications for iTec. My work moves between print and the web, from a poster on a wall to the way a project card turns under your cursor.
